## How the score is calculated

| Step | Points | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Starts at | 100 | Every purchase begins with full trust |
| Refusal that decided it | −70 | The first guard at the severity of a decline |
| Question that decided it | −40 | The first guard at the severity of a step-up |
| Further finding | −5 | Every other guard above approve |
| Doubt | −10 | An unsettled check the customer's policy approved |
| Signal, normal | −5 | A signal a guard noticed without raising the decision |
| Signal, strong | −10 | The same, called strong by the guard |
| Cap | at most −25 | Further findings, doubts and signals together, so the score stays in the band of its decision |

Trusted from 70, needs a decision from 35, blocked below. A record without an evaluated check has no score.
